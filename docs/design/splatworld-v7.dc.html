Turn 7 · the foundation surfaces
Artboards for what PLAN-foundation adds: a seventh app, Flows (F7, its own hue), and four new parts inside Build — Symbols and Ground cover as tabs of Settings, Shape as a part of Land, product types and ports in Catalog Register, and live ports in Place. Chrome, type and colour are unchanged from v6; the only addition to the bar is the Flows app icon. Flows is a full-screen editor: the world stays behind it, dimmed and paused. Every refusal is the sentence the plan writes, next to the control that caused it.
