PLAN-editors.md drawn. Shape and Lines are two surfaces of the Build plinth and share one ground view, Blueprint: the land as white clay, contours on it, what you changed as colour, everybody else dimmed. Areas are drawn in Survey. The tool rail runs along the top-left, each glyph carrying its key; one box under it holds only what the tool in hand reads and the strokes. The corner card where the walking keys are holds Blueprint's own keys, the overlays and the view, because while Blueprint is open the camera is not the player's. Words at the pointer, not in the manual: 11e has every tag and every sentence after Save. Plinth: Place · Catalog · Land · Shape · Lines · Publish (1–6).
