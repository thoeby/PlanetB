Import a region

Point this at a GeoServer that holds layers you want brought into the world. If you draw in QGIS against this world's own GeoServer, you do not need this page at all — see docs/geoserver.md.

1 · Where your layers are

Either one is a list to pick from — nothing is typed as a path. The database option reads the spatial tables this server can already see.

The root, a workspace, or a full WFS URL — any of them work.

2 · Which layer is what

Leave a layer as ignore and it is not imported.

LayerImport as Properties — your column, the world's property

3 · Elevation

Any raster with a coordinate system, in any projection. Without elevation, tiles cannot compile.

4 · Region and owner

Filled in from the layers you picked. Shrink it to compile less: every extra tile is real work in your browser.
