Import a region

Point this at your GeoServer, say which layer is which, and import. Nothing here needs a config file.

1 · Your GeoServer

The root, a workspace, or a full WFS URL — any of them work.

2 · Which layer is what

Leave a layer as ignore and it is not imported.

LayerImport as Height/width column

3 · Elevation

Any raster with a coordinate system, in any projection. Without elevation, tiles cannot compile.

4 · Region and owner

Filled in from the layers you picked. Shrink it to compile less: every extra tile is real work in your browser.
