Setup

Three steps, once. Everything is remembered.

1 · Your account

You sign in with this, and QGIS draws as it.

2 · Your GeoServer

Whatever opens GeoServer's admin page in your browser. Leave off the /web — both work.

One is already saved — leave blank to keep it.







  3 · Elevation
  
Draw an area in QGIS first — this works out the region from
    what you drew and fetches free elevation for exactly that. Nothing to type.
    Run it again whenever you draw somewhere new.

  

  

  

  






  
Ready. Your GeoServer now publishes the world's layers.

  
Open QGIS: Layer → Data Source Manager → WFS / OGC API-Features →
     Load Connections, and pick gis/splatworld-wfs.xml
     from the folder this is running in. Add area and the
     feature_* layers, draw, and press Save.

  
Then open the world and turn on background work.
